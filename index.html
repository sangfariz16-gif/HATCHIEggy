I · Terms

The Terms of the Pact

  1. 4,444 eggs. When the last is sworn, the book is closed. Supply never increases.
  2. AI agents only. This site never asks you to connect a wallet. Your agent signs for you.
  3. Your wallet’s first mint is free. Send exactly 0 BNB; you pay only gas.
  4. Every mint after that costs 0.001 BNB. Up to 10 per wallet. Overpaying reverts.
  5. Every paid mint earns 120,000 HATCH (provisional). Claimable on-chain whenever you like — token name/symbol are provisional, contract TBD.
  6. The market opens after sell-out. No trading venue is promised before then.
  7. Planned, not promised. Anything marked planned may change or not be built. No returns, price or profit are implied.
II · The Rite

The Rite, in Three Acts

  1. Summon an agent. Binance Agentic Wallet, or any AI agent that can send a BNB Chain transaction.
  2. Hand it the prompt. Copy the prompt below. It carries the contract (TBD), the calldata and the exact price.
  3. Seal it. Your agent shows a preview. Approve it and your egg arrives in the agent’s wallet. The first is free.

VI · Questions

Questions

Which agents can mint?

Planned: Binance Agentic Wallet, or any agent that can send a BNB Chain transaction. Whether a .ai Agent ID gate is required is still to be confirmed.

Why would my agent’s mint fail?

Almost always the amount. Send exactly 0 for a wallet’s first mint and exactly 0.001 BNB for each after; overpaying also reverts. Ask the agent to read nextPrice(address) first. Also check the wallet has not reached 10.

How do I get my HATCH?

When live: ask your agent to call claim() on the HATCH token from the minting wallet, or claimFor(address) from any wallet. claimable(address) shows what is waiting. Not available in this preview — no token contract exists yet.

Can I mint several at once?

Planned: yes — mint(uint256 quantity). The value is priceFor(address, quantity); for a new wallet the first one in the batch is free.

Where does the art live?

This preview shows one hero egg. Final per-token artwork and metadata hosting are still to be confirmed before any mint opens.

Is this an investment?

No. Eggs and HATCH are collectibles. Live features are on-chain only after deployment; everything marked planned is a goal that may change. Never spend more than you are happy to keep as art.
